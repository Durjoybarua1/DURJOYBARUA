About

Rooted in melody,
built for collaboration.

"Every song starts somewhere real — a forest, a memory, a feeling that hasn't found words yet."

Durjoy Barua is a singer, musician, and composer writing music that carries the texture of home — folk instrumentation, original melody, and a voice shaped by the land it comes from.

Whether it's an original beat for your next release or a full composition for your project, Durjoy works closely with artists, filmmakers, and creators to build something that fits the story being told.
